01 — About

Somewhere between a petri dish and a keyboard

I'm Roham, a biology-track student in the NODET (SAMPAD) system in Tehran — a national program for students who lean hard into science early. Biology is where I spend most of my attention: how cells organize themselves, how genetics quietly writes the instructions for everything alive.

Outside of biology, I've been teaching myself programming, mostly because I like the same thing about code that I like about biology: small, precise rules producing something much bigger than themselves. I'm also increasingly interested in dentistry — a field that sits right at the intersection of biological knowledge and exact, hands-on craft. I'm based in Tehran for now, though I'm keeping an open mind about studying abroad down the line.
